Work

Five projects. Everything here is nitinol or its high-temperature cousin NiTiHf, printed on a laser powder bed and pushed until it behaved or explained itself.

  • Beyond one material per functionFunctionally graded NiTi, printed so a single part carries different transformation behaviour in different regions. One build, no assembly.Additive Manufacturing, 2026
  • A seal that closes itselfNiTi lattice structures and shape memory sealing rings for radioactive material containment, developed with Los Alamos National Laboratory.Materials & Design, submitted
  • Surfaces that grip on commandSuperelastic and active interlocking metasurfaces: printed surfaces that lock to each other and release when heated, developed with Sandia National Laboratories.Materials Today, 2026
  • Where the melt pool runs hotShort scan tracks overheat and long ones don't, so part geometry quietly changes your process window. In-situ monitoring, melt pool mapping, and a parameter set that adapts.In preparation
  • Qualification with 80% fewer couponsAn in-situ monitoring build strategy, portable across OEM platforms, that cut re-qualification coupons by roughly 80% while holding every mechanical and microstructural target.EOS of North America, 2026
