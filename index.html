Taresh Guleria

I print metals that remember their shape, and make them repeatable enough to qualify.

Ph.D. candidate at Texas A&M, working on laser powder bed fusion of NiTi and NiTiHf. Finishing in December 2026.

See the work Résumé (PDF)

I work in Dr. Alaa Elwany's group in Industrial and Systems Engineering at Texas A&M. Most of what I do is getting nitinol to print reliably, then proving what came out of the machine.

Seven peer-reviewed papers so far, and two more on the way. I've worked with EOS, Oak Ridge, Los Alamos and Sandia.
